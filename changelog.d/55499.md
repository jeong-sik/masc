### Added

- Attachments accept PDF, HWP, HWPX, DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, PPT and PPTX, told apart by structure. An encrypted
  document other than a PDF is refused at upload with 415 `encrypted_document` (#55499).

### Changed

- A Keeper reads a non-PDF document as text on every runtime, including one that takes documents, because a
  provider document block carries PDF only. Until a reader exists it sees a `no_matching_reader` marker. Such a
  document does not move a turn to a document-capable runtime, and one that reaches the provider check unread is
  refused there (#55499).
