### Fixed

- The board attention quarantine inventory reads the partition ledger for keepers with a recorded requeue. A requeue whose partition is still `Blocked` or whose row is absent shows as `requeue_unfinished` (still a `Q`/`B` target, which finishes it) instead of `requeued`, and a requeue the partition ledger contradicts shows as `requeue_unreconciled`. An unreadable partition ledger adds a `partition_ledger_unavailable` error. (#44259)
