A turn's rows are drawn from one source at a time. When a log comes to stand for its turn, the committed rows it draws itself leave the timeline (rows_the_logs_do_not_draw); a Working log that can no longer end — journal unavailable, or a reply or failure on record — leaves the observed set and the committed rows stand for the turn. Ctrl-F moves the origin between its own row (this diagram's heading), the inline gutter with a short clock, and the bare gutter.
