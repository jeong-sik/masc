### Changed

- The server probes the runtimes on its own clock: at boot, every `dashboard_runtime_probe_cache_ttl_sec`, and at once when a forced read past the recent window asks; no read runs a probe. A probe a read asked for is announced as `runtime_probe`, a periodic one when its findings differ from the last probe. The TUI Runtime surface reads again on `runtime_probe`, provider usage, provider standing and `runtime_config` pushes (#48119).
