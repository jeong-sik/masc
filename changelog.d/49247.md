### Fixed

- The provider quota window store no longer writes a window its reader would
  reject, such as a reset that is not a finite number or a scope with an empty
  name. Such a window is logged and kept in memory only, so it is not rejected
  again on every boot (#49247).
- A provider quota window noted by a turn that is cancelled right after its
  refusal is still written to the store, and noting it no longer raises the
  cancellation out of the store's append (#49247).
