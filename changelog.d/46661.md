### Changed

- The Keepers screen follows the fleet stream (`GET /api/v1/stream/fleet`) instead of reading `/health?full=1` on every refresh. A change of the fleet reading reaches the screen when the server stores it, not at the next 2-second refresh. The header badge no longer carries the fleet reading progress text, and the `fleet reading: stale` line appears only while the server's scan is failing or the connection is down. A server without the stream route answers 404, and the screen shows one `fleet:` line and retries every 2 seconds. There is no fallback to polling (#46661).

### Removed

- The TUI's `/health?full=1` fleet read (`fetch_fleet_safety` with its dataset, read-source and need flag) and the fleet types and decoders in `Tui_decode` are removed. `Fleet_stream_health_section` reads the same section on the server side (#46661).
