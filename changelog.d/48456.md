### Added

- The `?` help sheet takes a `/` filter. Typing after `/` leaves the key rows, sections and slash commands whose name or description holds the text, ignoring case. `Enter` keeps the filter and gives `j`/`k` back to the sheet, `Esc` clears it, and a second `Esc` closes the sheet. While the text is typed, `h`, `j`, `g` and `?` belong to it, and a paste goes in on one line. The title shows the filter and the hint row shows the keys that work in that state (#48456).

### Documentation

- The TUI guide names the `/` filter on the help sheet (#48456).
