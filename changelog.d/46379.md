### Added

- `masc oauth-client list` shows the clients Dynamic Client Registration
  stored and the count against `MASC_OAUTH_MAX_CLIENTS`, and
  `masc oauth-client revoke <client_id>` revokes one with its tokens and frees
  its slot (#46379).
  Usage: `masc oauth-client list`
  Usage: `masc oauth-client revoke <client_id>`
  Condition: when the store already holds `MASC_OAUTH_MAX_CLIENTS` clients,
  new registrations are refused until `revoke` frees a slot.
