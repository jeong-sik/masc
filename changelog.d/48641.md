### Documentation

- The shares of Keeper turn time in `docs/evidence/execute-waits-2026-11/`, `docs/evidence/lane-time-2026-11/`, the tool-efficiency report and RFC-0379 section 6 now count only the part of a call that lies inside a recorded turn interval. 22.3 % of `Execute` time lies outside the turn intervals of the Keeper that made the call, and 24.72 of those 26.16 hours belong to turns that have no trace file, so dividing all of it by the turn time overstated the share (#48641).
- `measure_lane_time.py` gains `turn_time.inside_turns` (tool, gap, reread, long and polling seconds inside the turn intervals, and the per-Keeper polling split that used to sit in `turn_time.long_execute`), `turn_time.calls_without_trace_file` (calls whose turn has no trace file, split by `turn_kind`, by Keepers with and without any trace file, and by the set of `turn_kind` values a turn carries) and `turn_time.traced_calls_outside_turns` (calls whose turn has a trace file but that lie outside the turn intervals; all of them are `direct` calls and none lies partly inside). The ledger-wide `turn_time.other_turn_time_s`, which mixed seconds inside and outside the intervals, is removed. `measure_execute_waits.py` reads the intervals with the same code, both scripts have tests for the new blocks, and RFC-0379 section 6 states which tokens can read a run and that a finished attempt may carry no conclusion (#48641).
