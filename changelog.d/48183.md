### Removed

- The TUI's `--refresh` option and its footer `Refresh` fact. Every surface reads when the observer feed says its data moved; the loop keeps only a local clock that expires consent deadlines, redraws relative times and This-TUI logs, and takes the browser lane mirror frame until RFC-0482 sends frames. `masc_tui --refresh N` now exits with an unknown-option error, so launchers that pass it must drop it (#48183).
