### Fixed

- The TUI chat alphaTex preview reads `-` in the fret position as a tie, the way alphaTab 1.8.4 does, instead of failing the whole score with `"-.4.8" is not a note`. A tie holds the latest earlier note on its string within its bar and the three before it, counted in alphaTab's bars, and draws as `(7)`. A tie with no note to hold, or one that holds a dead note, shows the reason instead of the open string alphaTab would use (#44832).
