### Documentation

- The glossary now defines three terms that documents used but the glossary did not: `Report Artifact` (an artifact published through `keeper_artifact_publish` and reopened at a stable dashboard link, revised only with `id` plus `expected_revision`), `Agent Input Wait` (the owned `(run_id, request)` an `Agent_core.Agent` keeps while it waits for input, settled as Answer, Declined or Timeout), and `Browser Action Catalog` (`Browser_capabilities.catalog`, the public tool operation names and their Available, Conditional or Unsupported support). Each entry states its neighbours: Board Attachment, Wiki page and Evidence Reference for the first, `Ask` for the second, and `Browser_lane.live_capability` for the third (#44373).
- The glossary's `Region` entry now says in one sentence that a `loop region` is not a separate concept but a Region with the repetition attribute (#44373).
