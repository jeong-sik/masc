### Changed

- The Keepers age view draws the 24 fact-age bins on a log axis, 30 days on
  the left and under an hour on the right, adding two or four bins to a cell
  on a narrow list. A cell is coloured by the newest bin it adds, the header
  marks 1h 6h 1d 7d 30d, and an age group title draws the sum of its Keepers
  (#50611).
- The Story and Memory screens and the Detailed `facts` cell count facts in
  four groups named by the real bin edges: over 9d, 27h–9d, 1–27h and under
  1h (#50611).
