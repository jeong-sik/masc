### Documentation

- RFC `librarian-durable-message-floor`: §3.8 records how the L3 layer is split into L3a to L3e (message-floor state machine, Runtime step entry, step request, step gate journal with its driver, `consume_one` entry) and what the first two layers settled. Four points stay open: the served slot of a durable step, the cost of one gate journal per step, the starting current Memory when a chain is rebuilt, and how the host's Exact authorization and the wake discovery learn to recognize a step (#46581).
