### Changed

- **keeper**: `keeper_skill` reads a Skill by the name its Available list prints. A row of the list is the name and its description, where it was `{source_id, package_id, name}` and the description; a stored 65-row schema goes from 31,993 to 25,995 bytes. `identity.source_id` and `identity.package_id` are optional: a field the call writes is still matched exactly and refused on a mismatch, and a name the list holds twice is refused with the references to choose from. A refusal prints the references carried under that name, or the carried names when no Skill has it, where it printed every carried reference (13,551 bytes at the median of 55 refusals, 2026-11-21..27). Of 56 refused calls in those seven days, 48 wrote the right name beside another `source_id` or `package_id`. A call that passes the whole reference reads as before (#55447).
