### Changed

- The Keeper detail's Automation tab draws its schedules as a table with named, coloured columns instead of one plain line per request: the state's mark and word in the state's colour, when the last wake actually started, what became of it, when the keeper's ledger saw the stimulus arrive, the recurrence, who filed the request and when, and the payload summary. The page's closed history sits under a labelled rule, and a narrow terminal gives the columns up in a stated order while keeping the mark, the state, the two clocks and the summary (#40753).

### Removed

- A recurrence longer than its column no longer pushes a second continuation line into the tab; the recurrence folds inside its own cell the way the Schedules list's does (#40753).
