### Fixed

- Board attention no longer quarantines a candidate when every slot of its
  lane refused for the slot's own binding (quota, rate limit, an unavailable
  slot). The partition returns to Ready, the candidate stays Pending, and the
  next wake judges it. A process restart likewise returns a cut judgment run to
  Ready instead of quarantining it (#39186).

### Upgrade notes

- Candidates already quarantined as `exact_lane_exhausted` or
  `exact_execution_interrupted` stay quarantined; requeue them from the Keeper
  Info tab (`Q`) or the board-attention quarantine recovery endpoint (#39186).
