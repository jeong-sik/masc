### Changed

- HTTP reads get their own executor pool. Work that a GET or HEAD handler puts on a pool runs there instead of waiting in the shared pool's queue behind background jobs; all other work keeps the shared pool. The read pool has 2 domains on hosts with at least 8 cores and 1 below that, taken from the shared pool so the process adds no domains. `/health` `scheduler.read_pool_domains` reports it. (#46374)
