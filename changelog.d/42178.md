### Added

- When a Keeper asks for work only a WebDriver BiDi connection does, such as hover or drag, and no listed connection can do it, the MASC server starts the Keeper Firefox and its BiDi host and waits up to 15 seconds for the connection to be listed. It does so only when the workspace's `runtime.toml` has `[browser.live.bidi]` and its live lane is on, read at each request. The request itself is not sent on: its answer keeps its `error` and adds `bidiConnection` (the connection, and `started`: `firefox_and_host`, `host_only` or `nothing`) or `bidiStartFailed` (`kind`: `operator_needed`, `start_failed` or `not_listed_in_time`, and `message`), with a `retry` that says what to do next. One start runs at a time, and requests that arrive during it get its answer. A host that ends before its connection is listed is a failed start: the Keeper Firefox started for it is stopped and its record removed (#42178).
