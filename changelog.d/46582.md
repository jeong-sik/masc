### Internal

- Added `scripts/keeper-recall-write-reach.py`, a read-only report of what becomes of the claims Keepers write with `keeper_memory_write`: the write calls and their receipts, the candidates the Librarian settled with or without a target fact, whether the target was added before the write, where the target stands now after following revised and absorbed records, and the range of time from the write to the settlement (#46582).
- Added unit tests for the write-reach report (#46582).

### Documentation

- Added the write-reach measurement on 40 Keepers: of 734 settled candidates 485 (66.1%) got a target fact and 249 (33.9%) were closed without one. Of the 485, 12 (2.5%) kept the Keeper's own sentence and 356 (73.4%) are a current fact now or lead to one through revised and absorbed records. The RFC records it in sections 2.6, 5 and 10 (#46582).
