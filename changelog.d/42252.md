### Fixed

- HITL late-approval journal restore no longer fences the whole store over one unreadable row: a complete line from another or a future schema, non-JSON text, or non-object JSON is replayed as nothing, counted via `Keeper_late_approval.journal_skipped`, logged, and left in the journal so its evidence is preserved. Rows tagged with the v2 schema but violating its contract still fail the restore closed (#42252).
