### Changed

- `test/test_file_owned_env_reads.py` fails when a `.ml` file under `lib` or `bin` passes a file-owned setting name (`Toml_only` in the settings registry) to `Sys.getenv`, `Sys.getenv_opt`, `Unix.getenv` or another function whose name contains `getenv`, directly or through a constant bound to the name. The names come from the registry, so a setting that becomes file-owned is covered without a line in the test (#46858).
