### Changed

- HTTP reads get their own executor pool. Work that a GET or HEAD handler puts on a pool runs there instead of waiting in the shared pool's queue behind background jobs; all other work keeps the shared pool. The read pool has 2 domains on hosts with at least 8 cores and 1 below that. The server takes them out of its shared pool when it has 4 or more recommended domains and no `MASC_EXECUTOR_DOMAIN_COUNT` override; otherwise the read pool adds its domains. Agent Core's codec pool follows the shared pool's size. `/health` `scheduler.read_pool_domains` reports the read pool. (#46374)
