### Upgrade notes

- MCP schedule tools (create, update, cancel, note_add) now take the actor from the authenticated caller. If you passed a `requested_by_id`, `scheduled_by_id`, `cancelled_by_id` or `author_id` that is not your own identity, omit the field: the caller is the actor (#38054).

### Fixed

- The MCP schedule tools record the caller the boundary resolved as the actor instead of trusting the `*_by_id` argument, so an MCP caller can no longer name another actor on a schedule it creates, cancels, or annotates (#38054).
