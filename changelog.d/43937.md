### Added
- `[typesafeai] selection = "random"` draws a uniformly random armed destination per judgment (each of three drawn first with probability 1/3; a draw, not a round-robin), and a destination may omit `api_key_env` to be asked with no Authorization header — the local Ollama joins the walk keyless and arms the lane on its own. #43937

### Documentation
- See the [`selection` guide](../docs/guides/typesafeai-selection.md) for turning it on (binary first — an older server refuses the new keys at load), the destination example, what failure looks like, how to turn it off, and the measured experimental scope. (#43937)
