### Changed

- Cap the BiDi host record's unacknowledged results at the newest 64. A
  command that outlasts the server's timeout adds one entry each time, so a
  long-lived host rewrote a record that grew without end; the oldest now
  leave and the newest stay. A status sentence that counts 64 says the
  record keeps the newest 64 and nothing of what already left (#41919, PR
  #42009).
