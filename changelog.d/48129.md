### Changed

- The TUI no longer reads the workspace identity and a full refresh every two seconds. A full refresh runs only as a revalidation: at startup, on a reopened feed that missed events, on a recovered workspace, on `r`, and while the server cannot be reached, retried from two seconds doubling to thirty. A feed that cannot reach the server marks it unreachable, and an ended fleet stream job restarts after a wait (#48129).
- The removed cadence cases used a full-refresh health probe that no longer exists. The registered Keeper-schedule `1 != 0` observations are consistent with a fixture returning 200 without the required workspace admission receipt and the TUI retrying that rejected read: #48404 adds the receipt, and the recorded targeted run moved from two failures, including cadence, to five passing tests. The journal and confirmation cadence cases were green in the cited regression run. This change removes the obsolete observations but does not claim to repair those fixture regressions (#48129).
