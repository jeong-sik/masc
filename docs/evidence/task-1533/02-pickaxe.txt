    * fix(librarian): a structurally unprojectable slot no longer kills the exact lane pre-flight
    
    The librarian lane's pre-flight projected the request body for every slot
    in the ladder and failed the whole lane on the first slot that could not
    project. A failover slot appended for resilience became a single point of
    death: on 2026-09-11 openrouter.openrouter-deepseek-v4-flash refused
    projection on every librarian run (56+) while the two slots ahead of it
    were healthy, so the lane produced nothing all evening.
    
    The size decision is now made over the slots that can project: a refused
    slot imposes no request-body bound because it cannot run, it is excluded
    and reported (one WARN per run naming slot and reason), and the flow's
    own advance keeps handling a slot failing at dispatch. A ladder with no
    projectable slot at all is still a setup error, now naming each slot's
    refusal instead of hiding it: Exact_request_projection_failed carries the
    admission reason, rendered by a new Exact_output.admission_error_reason
    (exhaustive over the wire errors, kind names matching the evidence JSON).
