### Added

- The Tools page has a "도구 실행 통계" card. From `GET /api/v1/tool-metrics/series` it charts calls and failures, total time, and p50/p95 latency per hour (24 hours, 7 days) or per day (30 days), shows the totals as tiles, and lists the tools that spent the most time. Pressing a tool name narrows every chart to that tool. Buckets that the window cuts short at either end are left out of the charts and stay in the totals, and a bucket with no calls leaves a gap in the latency chart (#48248).
