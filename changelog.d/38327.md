### Fixed

- A lane head that failed with a timeout, 5xx or network error no longer stays behind its fallback until restart. The failure now records which Keeper saw it; that Keeper's next cycle tries the head again, a new failure renews the mark and an answer clears it for every Keeper. Other Keepers and the failed turn's retry still walk the fallback first (RFC-0458 §3.4, #38327).
