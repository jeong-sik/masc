### Fixed

- A completion review cancelled by a server shutdown before its reviewer reported a verdict no longer leaves the Task in `AwaitingVerification` with a repair warning on every boot. When every effect of the cancelled run is settled and it never invoked `report_review_verdict`, the review is retained as a definite refusal and the next attempt reviews the same submission. A cancellation with an unsettled effect, or after a report invocation, still requires operator repair (#49097).
