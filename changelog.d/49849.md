### Added

- `Runtime_vibe_acp_readiness` reads whether Mistral Vibe can take a lane call, over `vibe-acp` and without a prompt or model call: `_auth/status` for where the stored credential came from and `_account/read` for the account status, key scope and Vibe Code allowance. A credential that is not the keyring one, an account that is not ready, a key without Vibe scope and an allowance at 100% or more are each a typed reason; a budget Vibe could not read is reported as unknown. Nothing calls it yet (#49849).
