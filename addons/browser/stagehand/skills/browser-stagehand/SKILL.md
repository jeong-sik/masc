Model-backed `BrowserInstruct` `act`/`extract` require the observed
`lane = "stagehand"` and this installation's selected `stagehand` Exact
Output. They do not move an existing Gecko or live tab to Chrome. Availability
depends on the configured Chrome and the host model route.
