### Fresh state required

- Goal rows, proof records and Candle snapshots written before this release
  carry no typed `measure` and are refused as schema rejections. Stop the
  server and remove these files under `<base-path>/.masc/`, each with its
  `.last-good` mirror where one exists: `goals.json`, `goal_events.jsonl`,
  `goal_measurements.json`, `goal_verifications.json`,
  `goal_criterion_reviews.json`, `goal-verification-runs.jsonl`,
  `tasks/goal_task_links.json` and `candle-ledger.jsonl`. Tasks stay.
  Open Goals are registered again (#52903).

### Changed

- A Goal criterion carries a typed `measure` (`count`, `quantity` or
  `done_when`) and a non-blank `procedure` instead of the free-text
  `metric`/`target_value` pair, and `masc_goal_upsert` takes those two
  arguments (#52903).
