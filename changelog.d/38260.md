### Added

- A Keeper's Info tab in the TUI shows its Board-attention partitions that were quarantined at a restart: how many wait for an operator, why each stopped, how long ago, and the partition id. `Q` requeues the oldest one through the existing recovery route, one partition per press, since each requeue may send its judgment call a second time. A new `GET /api/v1/keepers/<name>/board-attention/quarantines` serves that Keeper's rows of the inventory the operator snapshot already lists, under the same public-read policy (#38260).
