### Fixed

- An Antigravity login names its account by its email alone. masc reads the
  email once as email text and no longer requires the CLI's `auth_method` and
  `token` keys, which it never reads. A login keychain item is read through
  go-keyring's `go-keyring-base64:` encoding, in the managed HOME check and in
  setup's import. Setup reports a private account whose saved login it cannot
  read as such, not as an unavailable private directory (#49313).

### Known issues

- masc cannot read a keychain item agy wrote, because its ACL admits only
  `/usr/bin/security`, so a managed HOME's keychain account is not compared
  with the selected account (#49313, #49243).
- An address Google gives to another account selects the previous holder's
  account generation, and that generation's managed credential stays in use
  (#49313).
