### Added

- `masc_browser_act` takes `set_cookie` and `delete_cookie` on the owned Firefox. A cookie is set for the tab's own page host (the domain is not an argument), lives at most 7 days (a session cookie without a lifetime), and holds at most 4096 bytes; the value is never returned. Live BiDi, the extension and Stagehand refuse it. ([#45279](https://github.com/jeong-sik/masc/pull/45279))
