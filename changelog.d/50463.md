### Documentation

- `RFC-size-refusal-starts-fresh-then-cuts-to-the-floor` sets one rule for size refusals on the official-client lanes: a refused Resume is followed by a fresh Start, a refused Start by the floor, and a refused floor ends the run, without measuring any size. The carrier and bounded-restart RFCs are amended to match, and the `SizeRefusalRetryAdmission` TLA+ model checks the rungs with one clean and six bug configs (#50463).
