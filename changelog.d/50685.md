### Added

- Observer and fleet stream connection lifecycles are now measurable from the
  transport logs: `tui_observer_opened replay=resumed|fresh|after_gap|reset_*`,
  `tui_observer_closed lived_s=<s> reason=<why>`, and
  `tui_fleet_stream_connection_ended outcome=…` on every fleet connection end.
  Reconnect cadence, resume quality and stream lifetime were previously
  invisible (#50685).
