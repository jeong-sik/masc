### Internal

- The deploy preflight and boot read one durable-store list,
  `Keeper_durable_store.Id.all`, and one table, `Keeper_durable_store.reader`,
  that sends each store to `Refuse_boot`, `Degrade_typed` or
  `Preflight_only`. Which stores boot and the preflight read, and what they
  do on a refusal, is unchanged; the goal store has no preflight reader
  (#39226).
