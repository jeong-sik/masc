### Internal

- A Lane Add-on package's exact-output request is retained by declaration id and request id before a call may be sent (a lane that is off or unconfigured is retained as a not-final attempt, and nothing is sent), and a request listed again gets the retained final result without a second call; a started attempt with no outcome is `outcome_unknown`, the same id with another body is `request_id_reused`, an undeclared output is `not_allowed`, and only an answer that matched the declared schema becomes `answered`.
