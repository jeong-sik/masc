### Fixed

- A lane head that failed with a timeout, 5xx or network error no longer stays behind its fallback until restart. The failure now records which fleet Keeper saw it; that Keeper's next cycle tries the candidate again, a new failure renews the mark and an answer clears it for every Keeper. Other Keepers and the failed turn's retry still walk the fallback first, and a one-shot walk such as a completion review records no failure at all (RFC-0458 §3.4, #38327).
