### Fixed

- The refusal of the account server over a Keeper configuration journal no longer promises a rollback. The boot does not read the journal. The engine would roll back only one that decodes and whose Keeper name and paths fit the installation, and would not start on another or when the rollback fails. Removing the journal leaves the Keeper definition and runtime.toml as they are (#53951).

### Documentation

- The interface and the setup guide of the account server say that `GET /health/ready` answers 503 whenever the authorization zone cannot be trusted, after the engine is ready too. The guide says the state comes from a log or a store that stopped after a failed write, or from a store and a log that do not match, and that only a restart reads the files again. The interface also says that the home and invitation pages still answer then (#53951).
