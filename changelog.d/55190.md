### Added

### Fixed

- Classified `.masc/media` as `Other_media_store` in `Server_tool_blob_scope.workspace` so `Tool_blob_sweep` no longer stalls fail-closed when workspace media exists (reference-based reclamation of `.masc/media` is planned in a follow-up PR) (#55190).
