### Performance

- A Keeper's recent approval audit rows (`Keeper_approval.Audit.read_recent`) are answered from an in-process index that reads each row of the store once and, on every read, only the bytes appended since the previous read. The read used to scan the store backwards, decoding every row, until it held that Keeper's rows; a Keeper with no rows in the newest day files read the whole 131 MB store. On 2026-10-28 that read was 6.7 s of the 6.9 s the runtime trust snapshots of 40 Keepers took; with the index the same snapshots take 8.9-364 ms, after a one-time build of 2.3-6.1 s per process. Results and refusals are those of the backward scan; `n` above 128 is refused (RFC-0474 L4f, #46672).
