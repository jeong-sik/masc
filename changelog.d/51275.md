### Fixed

- The guest `_build` cleanup passes over the exec agent (PID 1), whose
  `/proc` links belong to root once it drops to the Keeper user; the payload
  used to end in a traceback there, so no guest build output was cleaned
  after 2026-11-09. Any other unreadable process still keeps every build and
  is named in the report. A payload that exits non-zero is now reported with
  its exit status and the redacted end of its stderr, and a guest without
  Dune stays silent until an owned, idle `_build` needs cleaning (#51275,
  #50266).
