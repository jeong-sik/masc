### Internal

- Register every route in `lib/server` with `Server_route.*`, so each of the 386 routes in the route table has an authentication kind. 383 `Http.Router.get/post/add/prefix_*/ws_get` calls were converted (306 by `tools/route_auth_codemod`, 75 by hand, 2 `ws_get`), and `Route_access` gains the reasons `Browser_lane`, `Loopback_dev_token` and `Subpath_dispatch`. Answers do not change except for `GET /dashboard`, `GET /dashboard/` and the `/dashboard/` pages: the canonical-host redirect now runs inside `Public_read`, so a request for another host gets the 503 or 400 of `with_public_read` before the redirect. `scripts/auth-route-baseline/compare.py` compares two baseline runs (#46671).
