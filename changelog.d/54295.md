### Changed

- The Librarian reader no longer stops at a turn log line that is before the stored reading offset. When it reads a turn again from its `Opened`, a line before that offset that does not decode or is out of order is passed, and the turn's `Closed` is read without what the line said: with no Task when the `Opened` itself was passed, and not split at the baseline either way. A stop at an unreadable line now also carries where the line ends, the mark the units before it ended at and the SHA-256 of the line's bytes without its line break. A question shows a turn whose `Opened` was passed with `"opened": "unreadable"` in place of `runtime`, `task` and `stimuli`; a turn whose `Opened` was read keeps the same bytes. The `librarian.ask` prompt gains one sentence for that shape, so the front half of every question id changes once (#54295).
