### Performance

- `WebSearch` cuts each hit's snippet to a 1,024-byte preview. Ollama and Exa
  sent page text in that field, so a search without `includeContent` came back
  at a median 61.6 KB on Ollama; over the week to 2026-09-24 the same results
  would have been 2.85 MB instead of 23.09 MB (#38558).
