### Documentation

- The glossary now defines the TUI music surface and the three words it exposes: `Music Lab` (Dashboard `s` / chat `/music`, drawn by `masc-alphatex --music`, which needs the installed companion on `PATH` and neither Kitty graphics nor an alphaTex fence), `Phrase` (the piano-roll document that writes notes), `Arrangement` (the document that places retained WAV/MIDI sources as regions on one clock, with separate transports from Phrase) and `Take` (one recording pass, retained under `recordings/UUID` and selected with `:takes`). The entries separate the music sense of `arrangement` from its ordinary-language use and record that the surface has no `docs/guides` page yet (#44116).
