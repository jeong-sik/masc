### Added

- `GET /api/v1/auth/integrations` names, under `withheld`, the models of a
  subscription that a person is not offered, each with the reason. The account
  console shows the reason where a subscription with no model is picked and on
  the card of an account of it, and no longer lists such a subscription as one
  a Keeper runs on. The account server logs once at its start which providers
  offer no model and why (#52714).
