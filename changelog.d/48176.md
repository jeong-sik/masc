### Changed

- `GET /api/v1/keepers/skill-activations?keeper=<name>` now carries only the keeper's activation projection, while the new `GET /api/v1/keepers/skill-usage?keeper=<name>` carries the per-surface usage rows that L2 returned beside that projection. The usage route has `usage.state` of `ready`, `no_session`, `unavailable` (with `reason` and `detail`) or `no_catalog`, replacing L2's combined `no_ledger`, so a reader of every keeper does not receive every ledger (RFC-0483 L3a, #48176).
