### Added

- The Overview Team block reports each Keeper's spend over the last 24
  hours: a tag at the right of every Keeper row, and the team's total in
  the title. Cost is drawn only where some turn reported one, and a "≥"
  floor marks a sum that turns, rows or unlisted Keepers may be missing
  from; an unknown cost is never drawn as "$0.00". The tag is dropped from
  a row too narrow to keep its detail whole, so a stuck Keeper's cause is
  never cut for it. A stale answer says how old it is and why its refresh
  failed, and an answer the server has not computed yet is drawn as not
  read rather than as a team that spent nothing (#38363).
