### Fixed

- A broadcast or Board post no longer takes a dune alias such as `@check` inside a code span for its recipient, and a real mention written after that span is found. The regex `Mention` module is gone: `Board_addressing`, the grammar the Board and the Keeper lanes already use, now picks the recipient, and only a name made of letters, digits, `_` and `-` can be one. A mention glued to the preceding word, as in `Keeper(@name`, or followed by a dot or an apostrophe, as in `@alice's`, no longer counts as one (#46755).
