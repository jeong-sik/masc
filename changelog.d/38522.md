### Fixed

- A Goal proof review that ends without a verdict (verifier lane
  unavailable, a reply with no verdict, a refused commit) is now announced
  to the Keepers as `[goal_review_deferred]` with its reason. A deferral
  writes nothing, so nothing rescans the Goal; before this it sat in
  `verifying` with nobody told. No retry is scheduled: a Keeper calling
  `request_complete` again starts the next review (#38522).
