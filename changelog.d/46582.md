### Internal

- Added `scripts/keeper-recall-write-reach.py`, a read-only report of what becomes of the claims Keepers write with `keeper_memory_write`: the write calls and their receipts, the candidates the Librarian settled with or without a target fact, whether the target was added before the write, where the target stands now after following revised and absorbed records, which commit removed the id where the records end, the range of time from the write to the journal stamp of the settling pass, how much of the call table the retained raw traces cover, how many rows the admission queues still hold, the targets that may have existed before the write, and, as a base rate for the removed ids, how often a commit that removes an id also adds a fact (#46582).
- Added unit tests for the write-reach report (#46582).

### Documentation

- Added the write-reach measurement on 40 Keepers: of 735 settled candidates 486 (66.1%) got a target fact and 249 (33.9%) were closed without one. Of the 486, 12 (2.5%) kept the Keeper's own sentence and 357 (73.5%) are a current fact now or lead to one through revised and absorbed records. Only 217 of the 735 candidates have a write call in the retained raw traces. The RFC records it in sections 2.6, 5 and 10 (#46582).
- Moved the two prompt block ids of the Keeper recall design into the layer that renders them, because `prompt_block_id.mli` leaves out constructors without a producer (#46582).
