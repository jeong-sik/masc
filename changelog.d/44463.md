### Documentation

- The glossary now defines `Path Rest` (경로 휴식) as the driver's wait decision rather than the provider's observation: `Path_resting { release_at; walk_promotes_at_release }` and `Wait_until` in the runtime walk, `Rest_until_answered` when a quota observation names no time, and the completion review's `provider_rest`/`Reviews_again` reading that the same stores answer. The entry separates it from `닫힌 quota 창` and `Runtime Rate Limit`, and records that a failed attempt is not a rest (#44463).
