### Changed

- The schedule modify form fills its recurrence fields from the decoded recurrence, and a recurrence the TUI cannot read no longer opens the editor, since saving would write a default over it (#50469).

### Internal

- The Keepers TUI counts each Keeper's wakes of the next 12 hours per half-hour slot by walking its schedules' recurrences, leaving a slot unknown where a recurrence did not decode, the agenda read failed or the cut agenda stops. The grid draws these values in a later layer (#50469).
