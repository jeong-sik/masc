### Fixed

- The full-regression suite now prints its disk-band evidence (free space, the largest `_build` entries, `/tmp`, docker) to the job log while the suite runs, and the same file is uploaded as the `test-suite-disk-evidence` artifact on every outcome. Before this, the evidence reached the log only once after the suite waited out, so a run that died mid-suite — as `44801868642` did with "the hosted runner lost communication" and `44814053702` with "No space left on device" — left nothing behind to name what filled the disk (#54177).
