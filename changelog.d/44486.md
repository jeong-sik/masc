### Fixed

- A leader-selected CI dispatch that dies reading its evidence now says what died. `prepare-approved-batch.py` and `verify-approved-selection.py` print the underlying diagnostic beside the pinned unavailable reason instead of only `evidence_read_failed`, `SourceUnavailable` carries that cause, and `approve-guard.sh` names the failed gh read (review rows, single review, verdict, RC runs/jobs) before its exit 1, so a silent `set -e` death can no longer hide which call failed.
