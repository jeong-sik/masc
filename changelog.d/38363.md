### Added

- The Overview Team block reports each Keeper's spend over the last 24
  hours: every Keeper row carries its cost and tokens, and the title carries
  the team's total. A turn whose runtime reported no cost stays unknown on
  screen: "$?" when no turn reported one and a "≥" floor when some did not,
  never "$0.00". A Keeper with no turns says "no turns", and while the
  server is still computing the totals the block says so instead of drawing
  an empty team (#38363).
