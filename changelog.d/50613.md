### Changed

- The answer of `masc_board_comment` no longer repeats `content`, the text the writer sent in the same call. It keeps `id`, `post_id`, `parent_id`, `author`, `created_at`, `expires_at`, `votes_up`, `votes_down`, `audience`, `unresolved`, `comment_offset` and `comments_total`. The board stores the text as sent, and `masc_board_post_get` returns it as the comment's `content` (#50613).
