### Internal

- **Discord**: `Discord_rest_client.send_voice_message` posts one Ogg Opus clip as a Discord voice message (the `IS_VOICE_MESSAGE` flag, no content, `duration_secs` and a base64 waveform of at most 256 points). A reply does not ping its author. `Discord_voice_clip.of_audio` makes the clip with one `ffmpeg` run, mono 48 kHz 32 kbps Opus as Discord's clients send, and reads the waveform from the same audio at one point per 100 ms. ffmpeg reads one temp file with a named demuxer and only the `file` protocol; the directory is removed even when the post is cancelled. Nothing calls it yet; #55505 does (#55504).
