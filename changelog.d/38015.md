### Fixed

- The Task Review surface's VERDICT column read a field the verification queue has never sent -- neither the awaiting view nor the history view carries one -- so it was blank on every row ever drawn and the detail pane told every reader the row was `not joined (history view)`; live, the seven awaiting requests were all cancellations shown as completion claims short of evidence. The column now reads the closed pair the queue actually keeps -- a completion is written with no cancellation reason at all rather than a null one, exactly so a reader cannot mistake `not a stop` for `a stop that said nothing` -- and the detail pane draws a stop's reason under `WHY IT SHOULD STOP`, which is the whole of what an operator decides on a cancellation (#38015).
