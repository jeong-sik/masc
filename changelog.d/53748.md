### Changed

- The account server of a multi-account installation starts the engine once it has published its access: the owner state, its activation, readiness and the lanes after readiness, in a fiber, with the startup watchdog. A failure before readiness, or no readiness in time, ends the process. No server state is published and no gateway, gRPC, WebSocket or Dashboard loop starts. Logins do not wait for the engine (#53748).

### Upgrade notes

- On its first boot with this release, a multi-account installation writes what the engine writes on a single-user one: the refill of its config root (a missing `runtime.toml` among it), an Admin credential under `.masc/auth`, and the stores of the workspace under `.masc`. It still runs no Keeper (#53748).
