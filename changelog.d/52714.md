### Added

- `GET /api/v1/auth/integrations` names, under `withheld`, the models of a
  subscription that a person is not offered, each with the reason. The account
  console shows the reason where a subscription with no model to check is
  picked and on the card of an account of it. For a provider whose models are
  called, the account server logs once at its start that it offers no model
  and why, and `masc auth preview` prints the same. The reasons are described
  in `docs/guides/account-server-setup.md`, "6. Declare what people connect"
  (#52714).
