### Fixed

- A tool result whose parsed `json` holds `NaN`, `Infinity` or a duplicate key no longer makes the checkpoint save fail; the checkpoint never wrote that field, and the save now no longer inspects it (#44468)

### Internal

- The checkpoint codec encodes messages through its total encoder only; the per-block and per-message encoders that could fail only on that field are gone, and recovery after a refused save no longer clears that field (#44468)
