### Fixed

- An ACP Keeper at native `read` or `full` now runs its host in the Keeper's own folder, as Codex does: the host tree its sandbox mounts, or `<base-path>/.masc/official-clients/acp-workspaces/<SHA-256 of the Keeper name>` (mode 0700) for every other Keeper. Before, the host ran in the base path, so a Vibe `read_file`, or a `full` write, reached the operator's checkout without a permission request (#54946).
- At `read` and `full` an ACP host now gets only the `skills-dirs` folders its binding declares. Before, MASC also gave it the base path's `.masc/skills` and `.agents/skills`, which Vibe read, and at `full` wrote in, without a permission request; Keepers keep MASC's skills through `keeper_skill` (#54946).
