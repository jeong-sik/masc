### Documentation

- The glossary now defines the two nouns the arrangement editor leans on: `Region` (a placement on the arrangement clock — start, length, lane, repeats — that names no sound of its own; `plan.regions` and `umpRegions` in code, "regions" in the tool docs) and `Captured source` (the read-only, SHA-256-named copy of a take's four original files that `:import-take` attaches and `:open-take` opens). The Arrangement and Take entries now name `Region` instead of the generic "구간", and the entries record that the `take` in `:open-take`/`:split-take` names the captured asset, not a recording pass (#0).
