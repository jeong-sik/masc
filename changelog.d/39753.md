### Added

- Add the masc pad: `GET /api/v1/play/pad` answers the loaded DOS program's gamepad layout (Linux `BTN_*` button names onto machine keys, found by the program's saves name) and `POST /api/v1/play/pad {button}` presses the bound keys. `<.masc>/dos/pads/<saves name>.toml` overrides the builtin layout, which ships for `samguk3`. The play page draws the pad and reads a standard-mapping gamepad (RFC play-link-for-the-shared-machine). (#39753)
