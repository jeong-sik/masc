### Internal

- `Exact_lane_slots` gives the exact lane walker the slots of one lane and
  the visit of each kind. An HTTP slot is a flow of one candidate sent under
  the seat the walk took, recorded on its frozen runtime observation. A CLI
  slot is one official client one-shot run while the walk holds a seat of
  the client's account; a refusal of the account's standing notes a rate
  limit on the runtime's own cell, as an HTTP 429 does, and an answer
  clears it. A slot whose own declaration cannot become a flow candidate
  settles as that slot's failure and the walk moves on; only masc failing
  to give the flow an identity stops the walk (RFC section 3.3 Q1). No lane
  calls it yet (#54016).
