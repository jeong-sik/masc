### Fixed

- A microVM keeper's work volume now returns deleted space to the host. On
  Apple container the volume is a sparse ext4 image mounted without
  `discard`, and the keeper guest drops every capability, so blocks a keeper
  deleted stayed allocated on the host (about 330G across 12 keepers on
  2026-09-27). Each fresh guest boot now runs `fstrim` on the volume from a
  throwaway container that holds only `CAP_SYS_ADMIN`, has no network and a
  read-only root, before the new guest attaches it. A failed trim is logged
  and does not stop the boot once the named helper is confirmed absent.
  Unknown or failed helper cleanup refuses the boot. Cleanup also runs on
  cancellation, and long keeper names produce bounded, digested helper names
  accepted by Apple container (#39433).
