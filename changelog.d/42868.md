### Fixed

- One unreadable retained lane add-on binding no longer stops every declaration from being applied. The record holds back only the declaration that owns it, is listed under the configuration issues, and a record whose owner cannot be read holds back all enabled declarations. Retained records without `visibility` and `source_access` are now unreadable instead of being proven by the v0.48.0 compatibility reader (#42868).

### Upgrade notes

- Records written by v0.48.0 or earlier no longer read through a compatibility reader. Count the retained records this upgrade leaves unreadable with `rg -L '"visibility"' <base>/.masc/lane-addons/bindings` (files without a match). A detached unreadable record keeps its declaration applying and stays listed as an operator issue until the file is moved away. A record that is not detached stops its declaration until `visibility` and `source_access` are written by hand, and the detach tool refuses it too; `docs/guides/lane-package-activity.md` describes writing those fields.
