### Changed

- The browser capability table lists `read_frames` and `frame_interaction` for an ego connection: the viewport snapshot holds each iframe's content with refs, cross-origin frames included, a subtree snapshot rooted at the iframe's ref reads one frame, and `BrowserInteract` click/fill with `target` acts inside it (a ref from the frame's snapshot, or a role or text target ego finds in frames). A `framePath` on an ego connection is refused with that way instead of "does not read inside frames" (#45632). Guide: `skills/browser-lanes/references/advanced.md`.
