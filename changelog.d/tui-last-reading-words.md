### Changed

- The TUI says `이전 관측` where a workspace's identity was last confirmed but
  the latest read could not say, instead of `미확인`, on the status line, the
  Acting changes and the Memory world. The `/preset` and preset restore notes
  mark an unreadable preset `✕` and a restore that needs a look `▲` (was
  `!`), and report a disk sync or lock release that was not confirmed as
  `보고 안 됨`. A report the operator has not checked yet is `확인 안 한`.
