### Fixed

- A Gemini response whose `usageMetadata.totalTokenCount` disagrees with
  `promptTokenCount + candidatesTokenCount + thoughtsTokenCount` now records the
  disagreement as `inference_telemetry.total_token_mismatch` instead of leaving
  it invisible. The fold that puts thoughts into `output_tokens` rests on an
  undocumented convention; this turns it into an observation on both the
  streaming and non-streaming paths. Usage and cost are unchanged and no
  response fails because of it (#54178).
