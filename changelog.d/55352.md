### Fixed

- A Keeper turn that saved in its middle and then failed, raised or was cancelled now
  closes those messages at its end, so its turn log line names them and the next turn
  starts after them. They used to stay open and pile up in each later request until every
  turn ended in the same size refusal: on 2026-11-21..27, 1,922 of the 2,271 Agent Core
  turns that followed a failed turn started with messages an earlier turn had left open.
  A failed turn's tool results of 2,048 bytes or more now reach the next turn as markers,
  like those of any ended turn (#55352).
