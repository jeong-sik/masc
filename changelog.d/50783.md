### Added
- The answers of the multi-account PAT routes: a person with a browser session issues a scoped PAT with an explicit scope and expiry, lists their own PATs with their status, and revokes one. The token is in the issue answer once, and another person's PAT gets the same 404 as an id that names nothing. The routes are registered at boot in a later layer (#50783).

### Changed
- Revoking a PAT checks its owner before its revoked status, so revoking another person's revoked PAT is refused as not theirs instead of as already revoked (#50783).
