### Fixed

- Ephemeral per-request `Outside_context` carriers introduced by #54350 are now pinned in `Runtime_model_input_tail_window.is_extra_context` instead of being numbered as durable conversation atoms, stopping `Keeper_model_input_ledger.history_holds` from dropping the working ledger on every round. `Keeper_agent_prompt_metrics.remove_prompt_context` now strips both `System_context` and `Outside_context` carriers (at most one of each, checked via `Keeper_context_assembly.has_split_request_context`), fixing `prompt_context_presence_mismatch` attribution losses on `Outside_context`-only post-tool rounds and removing double-counting on dual-carrier rounds. `Keeper_run_tools_hooks` now scopes `split_outside_context` to `Masc_agent_core` so `Official_client` lanes retain outside-authored context blocks, and `Keeper_official_client_host.prepare_turn` fails closed on `extra_outside_context` (#54383).
