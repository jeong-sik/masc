### Changed

- After a size refusal, the request resends from where this turn began as the turn log's open `Opened` line records it, not from the start state the turn computed and checked by digest. A purge that rewrites closed messages keeps that start. With no open turn, a `Reset` after the `Opened`, a line of another trace, or a history shorter than the line, the start is unknown and the request is not resent from it (#53500).
