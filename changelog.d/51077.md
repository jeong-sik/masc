### Performance

- Keep each checkpoint message value's written bytes, digest and decoded
  record once per process, by message identity with weak keys, instead of in
  the memo a caller passes. A content digest or a compare-and-swap save of a
  history the process had already encoded used to encode, validate, decode
  and hash every message again: 335 to 454 ms and 105 MB of allocation for
  5,000 messages (11 MB of text), now 11 to 15 ms and 5 MB (#51077, #46410).
