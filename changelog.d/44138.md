### Fixed

- A streaming request whose body cannot take the `stream` fields is rejected before it is sent. `Http_client.inject_stream_param`, `inject_stream_options_include_usage` and `inject_stream_and_options` returned a body that was not JSON, or not a JSON object, unchanged; they now return a `result` with a `body_injection_error`, and the request fails with `AcceptRejected`.
