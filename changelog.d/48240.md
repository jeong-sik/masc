### Added

- `GET /api/v1/tool-metrics/series` returns the retained tool calls grouped by hour or by day: calls, failures, total time, p50, p95 and max per bucket, plus the 20 tools that spent the most time. `since_hours` (default 24, at most 31 days), `bucket` (`hour` or `day`), `utc_offset_min` (so a day starts at the viewer's midnight) and `tool` select the window. A bucket in which nothing ran is present with null latency. The read runs on the executor pool behind the 30 s live cache and on its own read-only SQLite connection, so tool-call inserts are not delayed (#48240).
