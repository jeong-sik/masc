### Added

- Each Keeper request records in memory where it starts in the checkpoint history, with its trace and the turn log's end at that moment; `Keeper_request_start.standing` compares it with the Memory writer's read end as `Behind`, `Not_behind`, `Not_comparable` (another trace, a `Reset` line between the two counts, or an unread turn log), `No_read_end`, `View_unavailable` or `Unknown_until_next_request` (RFC librarian-call-only-proposes §11.3, §12.2 W2) (#53956).

### Changed

- The official-client carried-front observer is one named type, `Keeper_official_client_host.carried_front_observer`, and receives the request's start (`~sends_from`) with the front and the bytes; the zero-history floor reports `Lane_cut` with a start past the whole history instead of no front (#53956).
