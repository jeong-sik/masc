### Fixed

- The Librarian's exact-walk failure line reports `outward_effect=started`
  when any slot of the walk sent its request. It read the slot that ended the
  walk only, so a walk whose first slot was refused with a 5xx after sending
  and whose last slot never connected logged `outward_effect=none` (#38525).

### Added

- `Agent_core.Exact_output.flow_evidence_generation_dispatch` answers whether
  any candidate a flow reached began its dispatch, next to
  `flow_execution_error_generation_dispatch`, which answers for the invocation
  that returned the error (#38525).
