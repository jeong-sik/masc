### Added

- `GET /api/v1/lane-addons/live?source_kind=msx_capture&since=N` answers the MSX screen with a machine change count and incarnation, or only "unchanged" when `since` is the current count. The count rises on every call that runs or replaces the machine, including a press that raises after running frames. The route needs read auth and writes nothing to the lane store (#38733).
