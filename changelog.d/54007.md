### Internal

- `Exact_lane_walk.walk` walks an exact lane's slots one per step. Before
  every step it orders the remaining slots with
  `Runtime_candidate_standing.order_exact` and passes a held slot as `Held`.
  It calls the first slot that can be called without waiting, whatever its
  kind, passes full slots ahead of it as `Seats_full`, and waits on every
  remaining source when all are full. The seat is returned however the
  visit ends. Until the hard cut it walks HTTP slots before CLI slots.
  `Provider_admission.try_first_seat` asks seat sources in order without
  waiting. No lane calls the walker yet (#54007).
