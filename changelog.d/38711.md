### Added

- `/team-cost` shows the fleet's 24h cost on the TUI Overview Team line,
  read from `/api/v1/dashboard/keeper-costs`. A sum with unpriced turns,
  unreadable rows or unread Keepers reads "at least $N" and counts them; no
  priced turn reads "cost unknown", never `$0.00`. Off by default, and not
  fetched while off (#38711).
