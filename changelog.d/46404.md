### Changed

- Fresh state required: a browser source is switched on or off only by its Lane Add-on declaration's `enabled`. runtime.toml's `[browser.live]`, `[browser.automation]` and `[browser.stagehand]` tables no longer take `enabled`, and a runtime.toml that still has one is refused; before deploying, remove those keys and the tables that held nothing else, and set `enabled = false` in `browser-<source>.toml` for each source to keep off. The Lanes screen shows a browser lane on while an enabled declaration serves one of its sources, and the TUI turns a source on or off from its package row. (#46404)
