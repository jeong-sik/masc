### Fixed

- A pool job that looks up a `Dashboard_cache` key whose compute is still queued no longer holds its worker polling for it. The first job to start the compute claims it: the waiting job runs its own compute on the worker it holds and publishes under the owner's token, and the owner's queued job then returns without computing. Before, when every worker was held by such waiting jobs, the compute queued behind them could not start until the waits timed out. A claimant that fails clears the key so later lookups recompute; the owner gets that failure. (#46389)
