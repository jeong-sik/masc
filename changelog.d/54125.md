### Added

- `/health?full=1` carries a `librarian` section: one row per Keeper's Memory writer with its state, the alarms on (`E1` to `E5`, `W1`, `W3`), their level, what is unread, the last asks and Memory. A registered Keeper whose writer is not made yet has the `booting` row, and a stopped store names why and the snapshot file on disk (#54125).
