### Upgrade notes

- Tool events, keeper wake payloads, verdicts and event-queue bulk backups
  are kept under `<base_path>/.masc`. With the server stopped, move
  `<base_path>/data/tool-events`, `<base_path>/data/keeper-wake-payload` and
  `<base_path>/data/verdicts` to `<base_path>/.masc/` under the same names,
  and `<base_path>/event-queue-bulk-backups` to
  `<base_path>/.masc/event-queue-bulk-backups` if it exists. Rows left at the
  old places are not read (#42789).
- The container images no longer declare the `/app/data` volume, and
  `docker-compose.yml` drops `masc-data` and `masc-oneclick-data`. Move the
  three folders from that volume into the `/app/.masc` volume before
  removing it (#42789).

### Removed

- `Config_dir_resolver.data_dir`. Each store has its own function under the
  masc root: `tool_events_dir`, `keeper_wake_payload_dir`, `verdicts_dir`,
  `event_queue_bulk_backups_dir` (#42789).
