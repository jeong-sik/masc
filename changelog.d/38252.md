### Fixed

- The gate pending store no longer keeps every approval forever. At install, a delivery whose grant is consumed and whose `Hitl_resolved` wake is no longer in its Keeper's event queue is removed, together with its replay outcome; an unconsumed delivery, or a consumed one whose wake is still queued, is kept. Rows already consumed leave at the first install of this binary; the boot log reports them as `retired=N` (#38252).
