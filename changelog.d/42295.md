### Removed

- Remove the dead explicit-acknowledgement path for direct native calls: the
  `Keeper_native_call.Acknowledge` change and its transition branch, plus the
  unreachable `Keeper_owner.acknowledge_direct_native_call`,
  `Keeper_owner_registry.acknowledge_direct_native_call` and
  `Keeper_direct_native_continuation.acknowledge` exports. No caller produced
  these; retirement of a Retire receipt is already resolved by the operation's
  actual Owner settlement transaction. The `Terminal_unacknowledged` state,
  its JSON codec, the Bind/Terminal transitions and the settlement path are
  unchanged; user-facing terminal-pending errors now say "awaiting Owner
  settlement" (#42295).
