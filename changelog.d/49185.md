### Changed

- A Keeper on the small input policy that can read blobs back now sends each tool result of 2 KB or more that belongs to a turn that ended as its marker. The result changes once, on the first request after its turn ended, and goes as the same marker on every later request. Arguments, smaller results and this turn's results go as before, an unknown turn boundary or a missing blob store demotes nothing, and the resend that answers a size refusal still demotes every result of the refused request. This is the exception `RFC-tool-results-age-out-of-context` makes to RFC-0363 §3.4.1 (#49185).
