### Added

- The turning imp now appears in two places. At startup the Overview keeps
  its frame and header and shows the imp where its sections will be until
  the first overview read answers; the first key the operator presses also
  ends it and still does its own job. `/about` (and `/splash`) opens a
  screen with the imp, the colour scheme and the Keeper count; Esc closes
  it. The imp turns only with colour on and a page the terminal described,
  and is held still otherwise (#39658).

### Removed

- `/about` no longer prints the ASCII logo notice (#39658).
