### Fixed

- The startup checkpoint object sweep keeps the bundles that turn-log
  lines a Memory writer has yet to read point at. The first unread line
  is the commit log tail's `turn_log_offset` when the tail names that
  turn log and the offset starts a line; otherwise every line counts. A
  turn log or a counted line that cannot be read stops the sweep with
  nothing removed (RFC librarian-call-only-proposes 5.6) (#54018).
