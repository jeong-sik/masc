### Added

- `masc browser-lane-setup --mode ego` writes the `[browser.live.ego]` table of `runtime.toml`. It finds `ego-browser` on `PATH`, then in `~/.local/bin`, and writes it only when `--version` names ego lite's and Chromium's versions. `--ego-command` and `--ego-space` name another; a table that is there keeps its values. `masc prerequisite-actions ego-browser` reports ego lite and, on a Mac, opens its download page (#45302).
