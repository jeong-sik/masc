### Fixed

- When a captured Read or turn endpoint observes that an Apple microVM guest with matching ownership labels is stopped, its cached work-root and shim readiness are invalidated before the unavailable-guest error is returned. A later owner turn can prepare the guest again. A missing or unreadable host identity snapshot still fails before guest observation. (#46924)
- Readiness invalidation retains process-owned cleanup and build-link effect evidence. Cleanup is reserved before dispatch and confirmed only by an observed successful execution, so stopped, absent or replaced guest names cannot automatically replay a possibly applied cleanup or link effect. Server restart and external instance or mount fencing remain unverified. (#46924)
