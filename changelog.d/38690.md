### Fixed

- The TUI Metrics fleet row counts a declared keeper as unpaused. It read
  `19 configured · 19 unpaused`, and the difference between the two numbers
  is what a reader takes for how many an operator has paused; the count
  skipped a declared keeper as well as a paused one, so every declared keeper
  sat inside that difference. `Tui_decode.keeper_of_declaration` writes
  `k_paused = false` and is the only place a keeper with that origin is
  built, so the count reads `k_paused` alone (#38690).
