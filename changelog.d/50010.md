### Fixed

- A Keeper can now send `take_over` with `lane=live` and no `clientId` to reclaim or reopen an ego task space its worker reported user-owned or missing. The Keeper argument parser used to refuse this call although the host accepts it, so the recovery hint led to a refusal. The host and the parser now share one route rule, and every recovery hint names `lane=live` (#50010)
- The worker's reclaim and reopen answer now carries its fields under `data`, like every other browser answer, so a reclaim that worked no longer reaches the Keeper as "browser success receipt lacks ok/data" (#50010)
- The missing-Space hints now say that only a Space configured by name is opened again. A worker configured with a Space number refuses the reopen (#50010)
