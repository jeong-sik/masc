### Added

- `set_viewport` on `masc_browser_interact` works on the owned Firefox: the window is resized to the asked viewport plus the browser's own chrome, measured again and corrected, and a size below Firefox's smallest window is refused with the size the page kept. Giving neither `width` nor `height` restores the window's first size. ([#45276](https://github.com/jeong-sik/masc/pull/45276))
