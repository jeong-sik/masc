### Internal

- `approval-auto-merge` now dispatches the main build right after it confirms a
  merge. Among the merges observed on 2026-11-10, the six whose `merged_by` was
  `github-actions[bot]` left no `push` run for their merge commit, so
  `Main minimal build` did not start for them and the build had to be dispatched
  by hand. The two merges by a Keeper or operator account that day each left one.
  This is a record of those samples, not a rule about every future merge (#51185).
