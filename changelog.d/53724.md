### Internal

- `Keeper_covered_point` reads a Memory writer's view as a place in the history a request is cut from: the count of closed messages becomes the atom that opens at or after it, with no digest compared. A view of another trace or a history shorter than the view does not resolve, a writer that has read no unit has no read end, and a missing, booting or stopped writer is an error rather than "nothing read". No request reads it yet. (#53724)
