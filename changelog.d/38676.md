### Added

- `Browser_stagehand_session`: one Stagehand v4 runtime over a CDP
  connection. It loads the extension under the id computed from its path,
  attaches to the service worker, checks the protocol major, answers
  `llm.generate` through the host, and sends calls one at a time; a call
  whose caller was cancelled blocks new calls and model requests until its
  reply arrives, and a model answer still being computed when its call ends
  is refused instead of delivered. A failed attach ends the session (#38676).
