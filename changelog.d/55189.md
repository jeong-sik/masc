### Added

- Workspace media upload endpoint `POST /api/v1/media` with raw-bytes SHA-256 addressing, magic-byte MIME verification (PNG/JPEG/GIF/WebP), explicit SVG refusal (`415`), pure OCaml raster dimension parsing, and single-user plus multi-account bridge parity (#55189).
- Immutable caching (`Cache-Control: private, max-age=31536000, immutable`), strong `ETag` with `304 Not Modified`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'none'; sandbox`, and `Content-Disposition` on `GET /api/v1/media/<sha256>` (#55189).

### Fixed

- Removed blind 24-hour wall-clock deletion (`cleanup_old_files_in_dir`) from `Keeper_chat_media_store` so workspace media embedded in Wiki pages and Published Artifacts no longer disappears after 24 hours (#55189).
