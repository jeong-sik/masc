### Added

- The TUI cheat sheet draws `[lines 1-16/670]` under its last row, through
  `Masc_tui_scroll.window_text`. The sheet is longer than any terminal — 670
  lines at 24x80, 386 at 60x200, since it folds to two columns when wide — and
  drew no reading of where the viewport stood. The row costs one of the rows
  it describes, so it comes off `help_viewport`, the pair the keypress bounds
  the scroll against; the drawing reads that pair too, so `G` cannot leave the
  sheet's last line off screen (#38622).
