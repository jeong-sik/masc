### Added

- The operator binds slots to a Lane Add-on installation's exact output in `[runtime.addon_exact_lanes.<declaration id>.<output>]`. The table takes the keys and rules of a built-in exact-output lane table, the lane is never required, and it publishes, loses removed accounts and round-trips through prompt presets like a built-in lane. Before, such a table was read as a `[runtime.<profile>]` table and ignored. (#44171)
