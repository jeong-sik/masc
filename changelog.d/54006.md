### Added

- An operator skips the head of a Keeper's Memory reading with
  `{"action":"skip_item","target":...}` on
  `/api/v1/keepers/<k>/memory/act`. The target names the first unread item
  (`head_item`) or a head unit whose content does not read
  (`unreadable_unit`), with the turn log reading stands in. The skip writes
  one `Skip` line naming the operator and cancels an ask that is out. A
  target that is not the head is refused as `not_the_head`, and the refusal
  names the head now as `now` (#54006).
