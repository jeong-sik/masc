### Added

- `Keeper_cache_outcome` derives, for one Keeper turn, why its first provider request read or rewrote the prompt cache: it walks the cost ledger newest-first to the newest earlier request of the same runtime, reports the idle time between the two starts against the lifetime that request named (outlived, within, or undecided inside the ledger's one-second clock), and compares the two turns' provider-input snapshots when both describe those requests; it stores nothing (#53952).
