### Fixed

- `test_encoded_pixel_snapshot` (test/test_msx_routes.ml) no longer depends on VBlank polling, which was flaky under CI — in failing runs the guest never observed the flag, pixels stayed constant, and the retained/inline assertion inverted (runs 46077144207, 46082771264, 46084052839). The synthetic cartridge now scans the keyboard matrix on the ocaml-msx port contract (OUT (0xAA) (0x50|row), IN A,(0xA9), active low) and toggles VDP R#7 once per edge-detected key press; the test presses space between ticks, making the pixel change deterministic (#55403).
