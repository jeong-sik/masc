### Documentation

- The shares of Keeper turn time in `docs/evidence/execute-waits-2026-11/`, `docs/evidence/lane-time-2026-11/`, the tool-efficiency report and RFC-0379 section 6 now count only the part of a call that lies inside a recorded turn interval. 22.3 % of `Execute` time lies outside every interval, and 24.72 of those 26.16 hours belong to turns that have no trace file, so dividing all of it by the turn time overstated the share (#48641).
- `measure_lane_time.py` gains `turn_time.inside_turns` (tool, gap, reread, long and polling seconds inside the turn intervals, and the per-Keeper polling split that used to sit in `turn_time.long_execute`) and `turn_time.calls_without_trace_file` (calls whose turn has no trace file, by `turn_kind`). `measure_execute_waits.py` reads the intervals with the same code. The tests grow from 32 to 63 for lane time and from 104 to 116 for execute waits, and RFC-0379 section 6 states which tokens can read a run and that a finished attempt may carry no conclusion (#48641).
