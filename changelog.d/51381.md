### Documentation

- Add RFC "keeper-turn-ends-while-waiting-for-a-receipt-state-machine" and the TLA+ model `KeeperReceiptWait`. The RFC lists every state and transition of a chat task that ends its turn while it waits for a background receipt, who owns the receipt, what happens on restart, worker exit, duplicate receipt and a receipt after a cancel, and two proposed hard cuts: the semantic record (v2 to v3) and the outbox receipt, whose `arguments` becomes `Known | Unknown`. A restart of a task that resumed from a wait returns it to the queue with its receipts, and the model covers that path. The model passes with no violation and fails with a counterexample for each of its eight bug models (#51381).
