### Changed

- The TUI reads the Lane Add-ons pane when the server says what it shows moved, and no longer every two seconds. The server compares the operator's Inspect every two seconds, per instance with its action receipts, and announces `lane_addon_instance` for each instance that moved, appeared or left and `lane_addon_configuration` when the declaration reading moved. The open pane reads an instance it shows, on a revalidation, and retries a failed read (#48158).
