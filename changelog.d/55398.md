### Added

- The TUI wiki card draws the embeds the server lists for a page. Image embeds (`![[media:…]]`, Markdown images of `/api/v1/media/` and `/api/v1/attachments/att_…`) draw as retained images in kitty and half-block modes and as their label otherwise. Embeds of listed pages become links that `<` `>`, Enter and Esc treat like body links. Artifact embeds and embeds of missing targets are muted notes, and embeds inside code stay code (#55398).
