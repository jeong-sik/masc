### Documentation

- Add report "open-issue-map-and-plan". It reads all 1,094 open issues and counts how many are labelled as breaking what the product promises (106). It tests those labels with blind re-rating: two judges rated 33 of the 106 as both observed and promise-breaking, and a body-reading pass over the other 940 issues found 37 more. It groups the issues by what they are about and what they ask for, traces the shared causes, compares two directions of repair for each cluster, records the result of re-checking 140 close candidates against main, and lists nine decisions left to the operator. The evidence bundle holds the fetch, measurement and counting scripts with their output and the per-issue verdicts. (#51363)
