### Fixed

- Refuse `keeper_candle_gift` targets that do not name a registered Keeper: the tool boundary now checks the recipient against the Auth keeper roster (the same source the invite flow reads) and answers with the typed `unknown_target` refusal before the ledger sees the name. Previously a mistyped or hallucinated name received a freshly created wallet with no clawback path, stranding the gifted Candle or item forever (#42254).
- The roster check matches a recipient spelling up to case and credits the roster keeper's own wallet spelling, so a gift addressed `minsu` reaches the keeper registered as `Minsu` instead of minting a lowercase wallet the keeper never reads; a name whose case-folded roster match is ambiguous is refused with `unknown_target` rather than guessing a recipient (#42254).
