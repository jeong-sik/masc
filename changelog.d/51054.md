### Internal

- `tools/rtev_trace/rtev_fibers` no longer counts fiber ids, cancellation
  context ids and streams older than the trace as held locks. On a busy
  server those entries grew at every `Get`, and a 30 s window took 78.8 s
  and lost 1.4 million events; a 90 s window now ends in 90.3 s with 85 MB
  peak RSS (#51054, #46762).
