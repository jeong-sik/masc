### Fixed

- A Keeper registration that installs its registry entry reads the durable
  event queue once more under the queue owner lock, before it lets go of the
  Keeper's lifecycle lock, so a commit that landed between the first read and
  the install is no longer missing from the entry until the Keeper's next
  commit. When that read fails, the registration is refused and the registry
  holds the entry it held before (#43529).
