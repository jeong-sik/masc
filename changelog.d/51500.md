### Documentation

- Rewrite the verification part of RFC "a-stalled-handoff-reaches-the-one-who-can-move-it" from a read-only recount of the operating install. Of 19 Tasks stalled in `awaiting_verification`, 17 stopped on a passing failure: the server process ended during the review (8), the reviewer's provider call failed (8), or a provider call failed after the verdict was reported (1). The RFC now proposes that one verdict report, counted whether or not it settled, decides the review however the execution ended; that an attempt whose journal establishes that no verdict was reported becomes a definite refusal, so the typed candidate failure decides the retry as the constitution states, while a review cut by the process ending goes to the next attempt as a cancelled one does; and that the operator list follows the `Awaiting_verdict` item of the task-lifecycle RFC instead of reading the run registry, which keeps only the latest 64 completed rows (#51500).
