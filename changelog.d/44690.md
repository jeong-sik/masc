### Fixed

- The microVM guest state probe no longer reads a failed `container inspect` as "no such guest". When the inspect fails it lists the runtime's guests and reports the guest absent only if the listing was read and does not carry its name; a listing that names the guest, or cannot be read, is a probe error. A stalled container apiserver therefore ends a teardown with `post-delete state unknown` and a start with `Guest_state_unreadable` instead of releasing a live guest's work-root and credential records or booting a second guest beside it (#44690).
