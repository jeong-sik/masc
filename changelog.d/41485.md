### Internal

- Board reads have their own error type, `board_read_error`: an id that does not parse, or a missing post. Reads are served from memory and cannot fail with I/O. `Board_core.read_post`, `read_comments` and `read_post_and_comments` hold the read logic once. `Board_dispatch.get_post`, `get_comments` and `get_post_and_comments` stay as one-line wrappers that widen the result to `board_error` for the callers whose error channel also carries writes (#41485).
