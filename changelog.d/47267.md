### Added

- Record a per-workspace claim on an Apple keeper's work volume. Dissolution
  attempts to delete its work and build volumes after the last recorded claim
  is released and no owner container is observed. Fresh Apple provisioning
  stops if the claim helper fails or returns an unexpected report. Unrecorded
  legacy owners and concurrent boots remain unresolved (#47267, issue #47237).
- Refuse claims markers outside the supported line format before adding or
  releasing a claim. The helper requires `cmp` to detect changed bytes,
  including embedded NULs, and refuses if that check is unavailable (#47267).
