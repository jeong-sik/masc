### Fixed

- The first frame of the Keeper execute output stream
  (`GET /api/v1/dashboard/execute-output/<keeper>`) now carries the newest
  2,000 rows instead of every retained row (up to 5,000 rows of up to
  4,096 B). `first_retained_seq` names the first row sent when older rows
  are left out. The unused `stdout_since` and `stderr_since` strings (up to
  256 KiB each) are removed from the snapshot (#55531).
