### Added

- `GET /api/v1/keepers/:name/portrait.png?size=N` serves a keeper's candle
  imp portrait as a transparent PNG (default 160, 16 to 512; any other size
  is refused with 400, a name that is not a keeper with 404). It is a public
  read like the keeper's other plain reads, carries a strong ETag over the
  PNG bytes with `Cache-Control: no-cache`, and answers a matching
  `If-None-Match` with 304. `Rgb_png` gains `encode_rgba`. The dashboard's
  keeper detail header shows the portrait when the keeper has no emoji and
  falls back to its badge when the image cannot load (#39715).
