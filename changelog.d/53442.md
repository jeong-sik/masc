### Internal

- Librarian rebuild L5h: `Librarian_writer` is the fiber that owns one Keeper's Memory store. It takes `<keeper>.memory.lock` without waiting and holds it until it ends, boots the store, and handles one event at a time from its mailbox (a direct write, a resume, an adoption, a retirement). Readers read a status the writer replaces before it answers. A writer that has ended answers that the Keeper is retired. Nothing starts a writer yet. (#53442)
