### Changed

- The Librarian is told which Keeper it curates for. `Keeper_librarian.input` carries a required `keeper_id`, minted from the calling lane's keeper name, and the `librarian`, `librarian.continuity` and `librarian.working_context` prompts show it as host data next to `keeper_instructions`, which is written to that Keeper. A blank keeper name mints no id: the durable pass stops with `Keeper_name_blank`, the continuity pass reports its input unavailable, and the queue pass logs a warning and skips (RFC-0468 §3.1) (#38378).
