### Fixed

- A Goal proof review that ends without a verdict (verifier lane
  unavailable, a reply with no verdict, a refused commit) is now announced
  to the Keepers as `[goal_review_deferred]` with its reason. A deferral
  writes nothing, so it starts no rescan of its own; before this the Goal
  sat in `verifying` until an unrelated wake, with nobody told. No retry is
  scheduled: a Keeper calling `request_complete` again starts the next
  review, and every deferred review sends one message (#38522).
