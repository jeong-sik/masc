### Fixed

- Previews that cut a string at a byte offset and appended an ellipsis now cut at a UTF-8 character boundary, so Korean text is no longer left with a broken last character; this covers 25 sites in `lib`, `bin` and `packages/agent_core` (#44457)

### Changed

- `Util.clip` in `agent_core` cuts at a UTF-8 character boundary through the new `Agent_core_strings.utf8_prefix` (#44457)

### Internal

- Added `test/test_byte_cut_previews.py`, which fails when a `.ml` file under `lib`, `bin` or `packages` cuts a string with `String.sub x 0 n` and appends an ellipsis or a truncation note, and `test/test_utf8_prefix_parity.ml`, which checks that `Agent_core_strings.utf8_prefix` and `String_util.utf8_prefix` cut alike (#44457)
- Removed five private UTF-8 boundary walks in favor of `String_util.utf8_prefix` and renamed `max_read_text_chars` to `max_read_text_bytes` in `keeper_vision_ingest` (#44457)
