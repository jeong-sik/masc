### Added

- Each Keeper turn carries the wiki table of contents: one line per wiki file (title, file, one-line summary), built from the pages at the start of the turn rather than read from `index.md`, followed by the request's route to `keeper_wiki_read` (direct, through `keeper_tool_search`, or unavailable). A wiki listing failure shows a fixed line without the IO error. Autonomous turns record the paragraph as the `wiki` layer of the dynamic-context receipt (#42961).
