### Fixed

- The board attention quarantine inventory reads the partition ledger for keepers with a recorded requeue. A requeue whose partition is still `Blocked` shows as `requeue_unfinished` (still a `Q`/`B` target) instead of `requeued`, and a requeue the partition ledger does not explain shows as `requeue_unreconciled`. An unreadable partition ledger adds a `partition_ledger_unavailable` error. (#44259)
