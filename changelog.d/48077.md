### Changed

- TUI copies (`y`, a drag selection, `/copy` and the other copy keys) go to `pbcopy`, `wl-copy` or `xclip` first when the TUI runs on the operator's own machine, and the notice names the tool that took the text; over SSH, or when no tool is installed or every tool fails, the text is sent to the terminal as OSC 52 as before, and the notice names the tools that failed (#48077).
