### Changed

- `GET /api/v1/keepers/skill-activations?keeper=<name>` carries only the keeper's activation projection, and the new `GET /api/v1/keepers/skill-usage?keeper=<name>` carries its per-surface usage rows with `usage.state` of `ready`, `no_session`, `unavailable` (with `reason` and `detail`) or `no_catalog`, so a reader of every keeper does not receive every ledger (RFC-0483 L3a, #48176).
