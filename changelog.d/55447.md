### Changed

- **keeper**: `keeper_skill` reads a Skill by the name its Available list prints. A row of the list is the name and its description, where it was `{source_id, package_id, name}` and the description; a stored 65-row schema goes from 31,993 to 25,995 bytes. `identity.source_id` and `identity.package_id` are optional: a field the call writes is still matched exactly and refused on a mismatch, and a name the list holds twice is refused with the references to choose from. A refusal prints the references carried under that name, or the carried names when no Skill has it, where it printed every carried reference (49 of the 56 refusals on 2026-11-21..27, 13,551 bytes at the median). In 47 of those 49 the name was in the Keeper's own list and `source_id` or `package_id` differed. A description that spans lines is printed on its row, so no later line reads as a row of its own. A call that passes the whole reference reads as before (#55447).
