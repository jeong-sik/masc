### Documentation

- Correct the scope of the `run.toolset` change shipped in 0.83.0 for `#54624`. When the MCP bridge offers no tool of the named server, the first turn fails with `invalid run configuration: unknown tool` rather than running without MASC's tools — the docstring of `muse_toolset_args` in `lib/runtime/runtime_acp_client.ml` says so, and the `run.toolset` table in `docs/RUNTIME-TOML-REFERENCE.md` agrees. The only MASC-side entry handed to `run.toolset` is the `mcp__<server>__*` glob, so `none` restricts to that glob alone and `read` adds the host's `read_file` and `search` (`muse_toolset_args`, on `Runtime_native_tools.Native_none` and `Native_read`). Which paths those two tools may read is the host's decision — MASC does not narrow them to the workspace on this path. (#54663)
