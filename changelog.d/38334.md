### Fixed

- masc-tui refreshes its operator token while it runs. A GET or POST answered 401 reads the workspace again: a different bearer there that verifies (another masc-tui minted it) is adopted, an expired file or one its credential record no longer holds is minted over, and the request is sent once more with the bearer held after that. A bearer from `MASC_TOKEN` is never replaced. Before, a masc-tui left open past its token's expiry was refused on every read until a restart (#38334).
- Two masc-tui processes starting on one expired token converge on one bearer: the decision that ends in a mint is taken again under `auth/masc-tui.token.lock`, so the second finds the first one's bearer instead of minting over it. A replacement keeps the role of the record it replaces, and `Auth_login.mint` answers `Error` when the bearer file cannot be written instead of raising (#38334).
