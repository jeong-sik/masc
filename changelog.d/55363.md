### Changed

- `masc_schedule_create` and `masc_schedule_update` now take a Keeper wake only: `keeper_name`
  and `message` are required, and `installation_id` and `action` are no longer fields of
  them. A Lane action is scheduled with the new `masc_schedule_lane_act_create` and replaced
  with `masc_schedule_lane_act_update`. One pair used to take either kind, so every caller
  was offered the fields of both: on 2026-11-21..27, 408 of 2,079 create and update calls
  were refused on that shape with placeholders in the Lane fields, and all 93 Lane action
  schedules Keepers stored over 2026-10-28..11-27 named an installation that was not
  declared. A call that schedules a Lane action through the wake pair must move to the new
  tools; stored schedules are unchanged (#55363).
