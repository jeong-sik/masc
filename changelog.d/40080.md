### Performance

- The TUI reuses the grapheme layout of a text it laid out in the current or the previous frame instead of splitting the text into clusters again. While a keeper turn runs the TUI redraws every 150 ms, and splitting non-ASCII text was 14% of an idle TUI's busy samples on its main thread. On the lines of a copy of the live board (1,000 lines, 684 of them non-ASCII), fitting every line to 80 cells took 2.986 ms per frame and now takes 0.469 ms. A text no frame laid out during two frames is dropped. (#40080)
