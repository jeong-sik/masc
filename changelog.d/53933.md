### Added

- An operator restarts a Keeper's Librarian reading on its turn log with `{"action":"restart_reading","observed":"<turn log id>"}` on `POST /api/v1/keepers/<k>/memory/act`. It is taken when the file's first line names `observed` and reading stands in another turn log, or at an offset past the file's end or not at the start of a line. The writer commits a `skip` line whose hole is `turn_log_replaced` (`from`, `to`) or `position_lost` (`offset`, `length`) and reads the file from its first line. Before, such a Keeper's Librarian rested and read into the same fault on every pulse (#53933).
- The act route refuses with `not_replaced` when that condition does not hold and with `turn_log_unread` when the turn log could not be read. A refusal changes nothing. A restart whose line is not appended stops the store and is refused as `store_stopped` (#53933).

### Upgrade notes

- Older builds do not decode `turn_log_replaced` or `position_lost` skip lines. As the last line of a commit log, such a line stops an older build's store at boot as `Log_unreadable`. An older build's `validate-stores` refuses it unless a `started` line follows it, which that build's Adopt writes (#53933).
