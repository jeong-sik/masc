### Fixed

- The web dashboard Keeper pane groups waiting rows of one kind into one line with a count and still lists each row's blocking reason (rejection, cancellation, quota return time) when a group has two or more rows. The opened waiting list scrolls inside its own box and takes keyboard focus as a named region. Lifecycle buttons are smaller, and memory fact tables stay inside the card. A reservation whose time has not come has no `since` in the waiting inventory (its request time moves to `detail.requested_at`), so the oldest-wait summary, the per-source `masc_keeper_waiting_age_seconds` metric and the dashboard's waiting groups no longer read a recurring schedule as a wait of weeks; a due reservation counts from its due time (#54930).
