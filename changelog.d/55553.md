### Fixed

- **Music**: `keeper_music_render` stops a WAV at the attachment limit, 20 MiB or about two minutes of music, while `masc-alphatex` makes it, instead of writing the whole file and refusing it afterward. The partial file is removed and nothing is stored. `masc-alphatex` gains `--audio-max-bytes N`, which exits 73 when the WAV would pass N bytes (#55553).
