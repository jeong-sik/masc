### Internal

- AGENT_CORE takes seats from endpoints and from schedulers the caller owns
  alike. `Provider_admission.take_first_seat` takes seat sources, passes a
  full source for a later free one whatever its kind, and when every source
  is full waits in all their queues for the first permit.
  `Exact_output.target_seats` gives an admitted target's seat source, and
  `execute_flow_once` takes an optional seat the caller already took for the
  first candidate; a seat that candidate's endpoint did not give is returned
  and refused before any callback or request (#53980).
