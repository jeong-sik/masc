### Fixed

- Task GC appends the tasks it archives to `tasks-archive.json` before it commits the backlog that removes them, so a crash between the two writes leaves them in both files instead of neither (#40427). GC stops with `Task_archive_failed` and leaves the backlog unchanged when the archive cannot be read or written. `append_archive_tasks` refuses an archive file that is blank, unparsable or has no `tasks` list instead of replacing it, and keeps archive rows that have no id.
