### Fixed

- An MCP `subscriptions/listen` stream over HTTP/1.1 now writes its frames
  from the request's own fiber. A notification is only queued for each
  subscription, so a client that reads slowly holds up neither the publisher
  nor the other subscriptions. A client that leaves a frame unread for 90
  seconds, or leaves `MASC_SSE_STREAM_CAPACITY` notifications unread, is closed
  and its subscription removed; before, its stream never ended and the bytes
  stayed in memory (#48849).
