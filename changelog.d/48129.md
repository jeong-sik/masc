### Changed

- The TUI no longer reads the workspace identity and a full refresh every two seconds. A full refresh runs only as a revalidation: at startup, on a reopened feed that missed events, on a recovered workspace, on `r`, and while the server cannot be reached, retried from two seconds doubling to thirty. A feed that cannot reach the server marks it unreachable, and an ended fleet stream job restarts after a wait (#48129).
