### Fixed

- The Keeper chat stream now writes its frames from the connection's own
  fiber. The Keeper turn only puts events in a queue of 4,096, so a client
  that reads slowly no longer holds up the turn. A client that falls a full
  queue behind is closed and reconnects with `since_seq`; a quiet stream sends
  its keepalive only after 30 seconds without a frame (#48844).
