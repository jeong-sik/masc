### Performance

- A keeper's event queue snapshot is parsed and decoded on the CPU pool instead of the domain that reads it, the HTTP domain included. Loading a 1.66 MB copy of the largest live snapshot held the other fibers on that domain for up to 19.46 ms, the whole load; they now wait at most 2.44 ms. The load itself takes about as long (median 18.31 ms before, 16.90 ms after). The snapshot's growth is #40074. (#40075)
