### Changed

- The server announces `workspace_dataset_changed` with `dataset` `provider_usage` when a provider usage report is recorded and when a runtime's quota or rate-limit standing changes, a reset passing included (the provider rest loop wakes at the earliest end). The TUI Usage view reads provider windows, runtime quota and provider history on that push, on `runtime_config`, and the history once its UTC day ends, instead of every two seconds (#48082).
