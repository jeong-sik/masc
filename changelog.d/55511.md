### Upgrade notes

- A Lane declaration without `operator_authority = true` has to name a
  `manifest_path` whose real path is inside the workspace. Otherwise its
  manifest is not opened, and the declaration is listed under
  `configuration.issues` in `masc_lane_inspect`. Add the line to an operator
  declaration whose manifest lives outside the workspace. Manifests that
  `masc browser-lane-setup` writes are inside it (#55511).
