### Documentation

- RFC `a-turn-closes-at-a-typed-end` proposes `keeper_turn_end`, a typed action a
  model sends to close its Agent Core turn. The other calls of the same response
  run under the existing rules, their results and the end are saved, and the turn
  completes without another provider request; without the action nothing changes.
  The evidence bundle `docs/evidence/repeated-tool-rounds-2026-11-16/` finds 41
  Agent Core runs in 2026-11-10~16 that repeated one identical tool call for at
  least ten responses (4,708 rounds, 50.7 hours), among them a reviewer turn that
  kept its slot 6.6 hours after its work ended (#52428).
