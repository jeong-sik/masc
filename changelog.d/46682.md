### Internal

- `tools/rtev_trace/rtev_fibers` splits each pool job's wall time (the sections RFC-0474 L4c names with `Eio.Private.Trace.with_span`) into the time its fibers ran and the time they waited, by suspend reason, and reports for the most waited-on Eio locks who waited, who held them, what holders waited on while holding, and what they did just before and after (#46682).
