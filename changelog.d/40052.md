### Changed
- `GET /api/v1/board` sends its page from bytes kept with the cached page,
  on HTTP/1 and HTTP/2 alike. A cache hit used to serialize the page and
  hash the body for its entity tag on every request (1.17 ms for a 288 KB
  page), and the HTTP/2 gateway computed its own uncached copy of the page
  on every request (#40052).
