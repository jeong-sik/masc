### Fixed

- A Keeper's free turn slot alternates between chat and autonomous work. When both have input, the line that did not take the last turn gets the slot, so an autonomous turn offered while a chat turn runs starts when that turn ends instead of waiting behind every message queued meanwhile; a running turn is never ended for the other line (#55307, #46120).

### Removed

- The `turn_slot_released` registry wake and the Keeper Owner's slot-release notification are removed; the Owner now holds an autonomous offer that has input until its turn comes (#55307).
