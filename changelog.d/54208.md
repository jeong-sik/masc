### Fixed

- TUI half-block mosaics now take colour capability solely through their projector instead of silently stripping RGB/indexed escapes via global `NO_COLOR`. On `NO_COLOR` or terminals with fewer than 256 colours (`Ansi16` / `Unknown`), MSX spectator frames and image/link previews now show a one-line explanation instead of emitting 24-bit RGB escapes or colourless half-block stripes, matching QR play cards and character-cell previews (#54208).
